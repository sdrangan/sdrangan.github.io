About the group

Next-generation wireless, from theory to hardware

We are part of NYU WIRELESS, an academic-industry research center researching next-generation wireless systems. Our work spans wireless communications, signal processing, information theory, and control theory.

Current efforts focus on the upper mid-band (FR3): spectrally agile large-scale arrays, co-existence between terrestrial and satellite networks, channel measurements and sensing, and agentic tools for algorithm-hardware co-design.
